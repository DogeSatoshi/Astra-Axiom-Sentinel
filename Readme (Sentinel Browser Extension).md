# Astra-Axiom Sentinel HUD (Browser Extension)

### High-Fidelity Tactical Market Scout for Chromium Browsers

**Astra-Axiom Sentinel HUD** is a professional-grade browser extension engineered to provide real-time snapshots of market structure and liquidity directly within your trading environment. It brings the core intelligence of the Astra-Axiom ecosystem to Chrome, Edge, and Opera GX.

> 🛡️ **Sovereignty & Privacy First:** Sentinel HUD is a standalone tool. **No accounts, no registration, and no data collection.** Every mathematical vector is calculated locally in your browser's V8 engine. Your strategy and symbols never leave your machine.

---

## What is Sentinel HUD?

Sentinel HUD acts as a **Tactical Scout** for traders who spend their time on exchange websites and TradingView. Instead of switching devices or tabs, the HUD provides a persistent terminal overlay that validates structural alignment and microstructure health in seconds.

It utilizes the same **Vectorized Math Engine** as the Astra-Axiom mobile suite, ported to high-performance JavaScript for sub-millisecond execution of technical confluence.

## Core Intelligence Modules

### 🔍 Market Structure Validation
- **1H Structural Trend**: Real-time monitoring of EMA 50/200 alignment and Supertrend directionality.
- **Regime Identification**: Automatically classifies the current environment (Bullish, Bearish, or Neutral) to prevent trading against the macro-tide.

### ⚡ Scalping Confluence Engine
- **Triple-EMA50 Alignment**: Simultaneously scans the **M15, M30, and H1** timeframes.
- **Confluence Reporting**: Detects "Full Confluence" (L/L/L or S/S/S) to help scalpers identify high-probability entries where multiple timeframes are in agreement.

### 🌊 Microstructure & Orderbook Intelligence
- **Liquidity Depth**: Real-time snapshot of the top 100 levels of the bid/ask wall, displayed in USDT value.
- **Book Ratio**: High-precision calculation of buy/sell pressure skew.
- **Execution Spread**: Live monitoring of the best bid/ask gap to identify execution risk before you place an order.

### 🌎 Macro & Breadth Context
- **Global Pulse**: Integrated tracking of the **VIX (Volatility Index)** and **US10Y (Treasury Yield)** to define the global Risk-On/Risk-Off regime.
- **Relative Strength (Alpha)**: Automated comparison against the Market Leader (**BTC** for Crypto, **S&P 500** for Finance) to identify if an asset is outperforming the broader market.

### 🚩 Red Flag Detection
- Automated alerts for **High Volatility (ATR%)**, **Volume Anomalies (Z-Score)**, and **Liquidity Skew**.

## Installation

### Manual Installation (Developer Mode)
1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open your browser and navigate to `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions`).
3. Enable **"Developer mode"** in the top right.
4. Click **"Load unpacked"** and select the extension folder.
