Privacy Policy for Astra-Axiom Sentinel
Last Updated: 2026-11-07

Astra-Axiom Intelligence Systems ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Astra-Axiom Sentinel mobile application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App. We have designed Sentinel with a strict privacy-first, local-first architecture.

1. Information We Do Not Collect
We do not require you to create an account. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

Personal identification information (Name, email address, phone number).

User-generated data (your watchlists, scan preferences, or results).

Location data.

Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, or advertising IDs).

Telemetry or analytics data.

No accounts. No telemetry. No cloud dependencies. No ads.

2. Data Processed Locally on Your Device
All computations, calculations, and data caching occur strictly on your device.

Market Data Caching: When you scan an asset, the App caches this data temporarily on your device's internal storage to improve performance. This data is purged automatically when you clear the app's cache or uninstall the App.

3. Third-Party Services (Data Fetching)
To perform market scans, the App must fetch real-time and historical market data from third-party public APIs over the internet. When you initiate a scan, your device sends a request directly to these providers.

While we do not collect your data, these third-party providers may receive standard network information (such as your IP address) as part of the standard HTTP request required to return the market data.

Binance / Global Exchanges: (For Crypto assets). Please refer to Binance's Privacy Policy.

Yahoo Finance: (For Forex, Stocks, and Commodities). Please refer to Yahoo's Privacy Policy.

Global Macro Data Providers (e.g., FRED): Please refer to their respective Privacy Policies.

4. Payments and In-App Purchases
If you purchase the Premium edition of the App, the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play Billing.

We do not collect, see, or store your credit card details or payment information.

Google Play provides us with an anonymous receipt token to verify your purchase.

Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for how they handle payment data.

5. Children's Privacy
The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
